AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version works with AgentTerm 0.10 on the Mac only: https://github.com/Bitfaring/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.10.0

- The Inbox, at the top of the sessions' list: the agents that wait for an answer, what stopped, and the agents that are done and were not looked at since. A done item opens what its turn came to: what was asked, what the agent answered, the commands it ran and the files that changed.
- The sessions' list shows each agent with its own shells and pages, then your shells and pages under a heading. Tab groups and their colours are gone.
- The agent's thinking level is shown next to its model, and each agent's session has a mark of whether its hooks and AgentTerm's server were heard.
- A session another device has says which: "In use on iPad".
- Fetch, Pull and Push are no longer held while the app asks the remotes by itself.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.10.0 first, restart its host when the app offers to, and set the agents up when it asks (Settings > Hooks & MCP). For Codex, do what its row says there if it shows "Shared server on".
2. Start an agent on the Mac and give it something to do. When it is done or asks something, look at the top of the sessions' list on the iPhone: its item should be there. Touch it.
3. Open a folder's Git screen, commit something and push it.

What to tell me

What did not work, what was slow, and anything that read wrong.
