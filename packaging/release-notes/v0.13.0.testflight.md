AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version works with AgentTerm 0.13 on the Mac only: https://github.com/manhpham90vn/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.13.0

- Read is the same on the Mac and the iPhone; tapping a notification reads it.
- Notifications come at the same moment on the Mac and the iPhone, after 30 seconds for a finished agent, and not at all if it was already handled.
- One Live Activity, kept up to date between turns, instead of a new one each time.
- A first screen that asks what may be reported, then for notifications.
- The Inbox shows all of what an agent said, and opens an item whole.
- Not Now on the first screen turns notifications off.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.13.0 first and restart its host when the app offers to.
2. Start an agent, lock the iPhone: the notification comes about 30 seconds after it is done; read it on the Mac before then and it should not come at all.
3. Run a few short turns in a row: the Live Activity should change state, not appear again each time.
4. Open an item of the Inbox with a long answer.
5. Lock the iPhone during a turn: the notification still comes, through Apple.

What to tell me

A notification that came too early, too late, twice or not at all; a Live Activity that alerted each turn or stayed at an old state; anything that read wrong.
