AgentTerm 0.14.0 speaks protocol version 39: both the Mac and the iPhone have to be 0.14, and so does the host. After updating the Mac app, restart the host when the app offers to, then update the iPhone app.

## New

- **Notifications come at once, by what they need of you.** An agent that asks something or needs your permission, one that is done, one that stops asking, and a failure that stopped work are notified at once on the Mac and the iPhone, with sound, and as time-sensitive, so they show during a Focus. Progress is not notified. The 30-second wait of 0.13 is gone.
- **The Live Activity keeps up**: an agent that starts or stops asking, or is done, changes it at once; progress changes it when iOS sees fit.
- **An agent's notice floats over the top of the window** (Mac), under the tabs, and takes no room from the terminal. A done agent's goes after 10 seconds, one that waits stays, and ten at most are stacked. Faults stay banners.
- **Worktrees are picked from a popup**, as branches are, with search, New Worktree, and each worktree's actions (Merge, Open a Pull Request, Throw Away…) inside, on both apps.
- **Answering an agent while writing a prompt** (iPhone): while an agent asks, Send keeps the prompt, and "Answer the Agent" opens a place to answer with the question's choices; you go back and forth between the two and neither text is lost. The Mac's "Send to an Agent" sheet keeps its note while the agent asks.
- **The Inbox shows the end of what was said**, three lines at most; an item opens whole, with Preview Markdown or Show as Text, on both apps.

## Changed

- History no longer pins or hides conversations, on either app.
- Antigravity's conversations are read from its own store, read only; AgentTerm never writes into an agent's store.

## Fixed

- After an update, the new app could talk to the host of the release before and show a notice of an older release that stayed. An older host is now named in the Mac's banner only.
- On the iPhone, a session's screen went wrong while Claude Code had subagents at work, until the session was opened again.
- The worktrees' control stayed away after the app connected to its host again.
- A submodule's own worktree was listed as its parent's `.git/modules` folder: on the iPhone, Git said such a folder was not a repository, and History and Files did not open.
- History listed none of Antigravity's conversations at 1.5.12.
- "Also While the App Is Closed" showed on while iOS had notifications off for the app (iPhone).
- A page watched from a device is sent sharper, at the width of the device's screen, and no longer shakes the Mac's page.
- The launch buttons of an empty folder, and the side panel's tabs, broke their names inside words.
