AgentTerm 0.15.0 speaks protocol version 40: both the Mac and the iPhone have to be 0.15, and so does the host. After updating the Mac app, restart the host when the app offers to, then update the iPhone app.

## New

- **A worktree being made is shown at once**, under its folder, on both apps, with what is being done: "Making the worktree…", "Checking out submodule 3 of 12: manhdev…", "Starting Claude Code…". The same for a conversation continued in a new worktree and a schedule's run in one.
- **A new worktree's submodules are copied from the folder**, which has them already, rather than downloaded again: a folder of many submodules took minutes and hundreds of megabytes a worktree. Each still pushes and fetches where it comes from.
- **Worktrees are named by their folder** (`apps`, `claude-1`), with a folder's symbol, and the branch checked out in each is said beside it, so the worktrees' button and the branch's no longer read the same.
- **Both popups, the worktrees' and the branches', have ⋯ on each row**, and the same menu on a right click (Mac).
- **A done agent's notice counts down** the seconds before it goes (Mac).

## Fixed

- The Mac app crashed on some Inbox rows, and the iPhone app when such a notification was touched: an answer ending in a long word was cut twice to the same text.
- A session in a worktree kept the branch the worktree was made with after another was checked out in it.
- In Commits, a branch wider than the panel hid "Commits of" and widened the branch's popup past the panel; a long branch's chip hid the commit's subject.
