AgentTerm 0.15.1 speaks protocol version 45: both the Mac and the iPhone have to be 0.15.1, and so does the host. After updating the Mac app, restart the host when the app offers to, then update the iPhone app.

This release fixes what a review of the whole app found, and a few things the owner asked for.

## New

- **What an agent asks is answered from the Inbox**, on the Mac and the iPhone: each choice is a button, and a field takes an answer in your own words, as Claude Code's "Type something." does.
- **A notice is read on every device once its agent moves on**, also when you answered it in its terminal. A notice also waits 10 seconds before it shows, and is not shown at all if you acted meanwhile.
- **Deleting a schedule and removing a paired device ask first.** Quitting with unsaved files asks too.
- **A pairing link opened from elsewhere asks before it pairs**; a code scanned in the app does not.
- **What cannot be done while the Mac is not connected is said at once** on the iPhone (a commit, a save, a schedule, a file), and what you were writing is kept. A message sent from a terminal is kept with the reason, and keys typed meanwhile are dropped and said once, not sent later.
- **The banners on the iPhone take their own room** at the top, instead of covering the list.
- **A host that cannot listen on its Tailscale address says so**, with the reason, in the banner of both apps and on the pairing sheet.

## Fixed

- A commit or a save on the iPhone could wait for ever when the connection dropped, or end before git had answered and throw the message away.
- A git question left open (Discard, Revert, Delete Branch) could be answered in another folder after the session in front changed.
- Open files were read again from the disk on the main thread every 5 seconds, which held the window with large files or a slow volume.
- A program printing without end made the host's memory grow without bound; it now waits, as in a terminal that cannot keep up.
- A browser profile deleted from the iPhone or another Mac kept its tabs open and signed in; a page's second permission question was lost, and a microphone question stayed after its page had gone.
- Text sent to an agent could carry control characters, such as Escape, from a page or a paste.
- A macOS notification of one Mac, answered while another was on screen, acted on the other Mac. What the iPhone kept for one Mac could be sent to the next.
- A Mac forgotten on the iPhone kept sending notifications nothing could open.
- A schedule "every N minutes" whose hours cross midnight ran once a day.
- A state file the host could not read was written over; it is now set aside and said.
- Several crashes on numbers out of range from a client, an agent or the host.
