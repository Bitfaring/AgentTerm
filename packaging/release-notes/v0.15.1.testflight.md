AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version works with AgentTerm 0.15.1 on the Mac only: https://github.com/manhpham90vn/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.15.1

- What an agent asks is answered from the Inbox: its choices, or your own words.
- A notice is read once its agent moves on, also when you answered it in its terminal.
- What cannot be done while the Mac is not connected is said at once, and what you were writing is kept.
- The banners take their own room at the top instead of covering the list.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.15.1 first and restart its host when the app offers to.
2. Have an agent ask a question with several choices, and look at its notification with the iPhone locked: are all the choices there, and can you still reply in your own words?
3. Answer an agent's question in its terminal on the Mac while the iPhone app is not open: its notification on the iPhone should go away.
4. Open a question from the Inbox and answer it in your own words.

What to tell me

A notification whose reply in your own words is missing; a notification that stays after the agent went on; an answer from the Inbox that does not reach the agent.
