AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version needs AgentTerm 0.8 on the Mac, with its host restarted: https://github.com/Bitfaring/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.8.0

- An agent's shells and browser pages are listed under it, and its page is shown live: touch it, type into it, change the address, show it as a phone or a tablet would, zoom, open a new tab.
- On the iPad, a layout of its own: on its side, the page beside the agent.
- The two-panel diff shows a diff whole, where it stopped at 2,500 rows.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.8 first, and restart its host when the app offers to.
2. Ask an agent on the Mac to open a web page in AgentTerm's browser. On the iPhone, open the agent, then Browser: touch a link on the page and type into a field.
3. On an iPad turned on its side, open an agent with a page: the page should be beside the agent.
4. Open a changed file in a folder's Git tab, switch to two panels and scroll down and sideways.

What to tell me

What did not work, what was slow, and anything that read wrong.
