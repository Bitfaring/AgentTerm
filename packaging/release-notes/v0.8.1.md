AgentTerm 0.8.1 speaks protocol version 22, as 0.8.0 does: a Mac and an iPhone of 0.8.x work together. After updating the Mac app, restart the host when the app offers to.

## New

- **One Browser tab for your pages.** The pages you open in a project are one tab of its strip, as a window of Chrome holds its tabs: a row of pages inside, a + for one more, and closing several at once is asked first.
- **A + beside an agent's pages** opens one more page in the agent's area: yours to use first, the agent's once you give it back.
- **Pick, on the page's bar.** Turn it on and click an element to send it to an agent, with a note; it stays on for the next element until you turn it off.
- **Show As, on the page's bar.** It names the device in use and lists the devices to find one in.
- **`shell_stop`**: an agent stops what runs in its shell in one call, and is told how it ended.

## Changed

- A page shown as a device is as wide as the device's screen and as tall as its pane, never drawn smaller; the rest is reached by scrolling. The iPhone and the iPad show its picture in its own shape, never squeezed.
- The browser's processes are named "AgentTerm Browser (Chromium)" in Activity Monitor, so that the memory they use is seen to be the browser's.
- "Agents can use the browser" is gone from Settings: the agents always have the browser, as they have their shells.
- The bar of a page you took over says "You have this tab: AgentTerm waits until you give it back".

## Fixed

- A closed tab's page process ends, and gives its memory back.
- A session started while a page of the browser is in front comes to the front, rather than stay unseen behind the page.
- A page shown as a device no longer reaches the iPhone squeezed into another shape.
- A word still being composed, as in Vietnamese, is no longer left out of the note sent with a picked element.
