AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version works with AgentTerm 0.8 on the Mac: https://github.com/Bitfaring/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.8.1

- A page shown as a phone or a tablet is drawn in its own shape, never squeezed: it is as wide as the device's screen, and the rest is reached by scrolling.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.8.1 first, and restart its host when the app offers to.
2. Ask an agent on the Mac to open a web page, and show it as a phone (Show As). On the iPhone, open the agent, then Browser: the page should not look squeezed, and scrolling it should show the rest.

What to tell me

What did not work, what was slow, and anything that read wrong.
