AgentTerm 0.9.0 speaks protocol version 22, as 0.8.x does: a Mac and an iPhone of 0.8.x and 0.9.0 work together. It is a release of fixes, out early for two of them: the browser's memory, and a session used on an iPad.

## Fixed

- **A page you close gives its memory back.** A page of yours that was closed was made again out of sight, and its process ran on, some 30 to 90 MB each, until the app quit. With several pages opened and closed over a day, that was hundreds of megabytes held for nothing.
- **No page process is kept in reserve.** With the last page closed, none of the pages' processes is left running.
- **A session in use on an iPad is shown whole on the Mac.** A session used on a device whose grid is larger than the session's pane on the Mac, an iPad's in the Shells part under an agent, was drawn at its own size and cut off at the bottom, the prompt out of sight. The Mac now shows the whole grid, scaled down to fit, as the iPhone shows the Mac's.
- **iPhone and iPad: a menu's entry is chosen when touched.** Now and then a touch on an entry of a menu in the sessions' list (End Session, Rename Group, Pin, Move Down) chose nothing and the menu stayed open: the list, drawn again for the Mac's figures every few seconds, had the menu made anew under the finger. An open menu now stays as it is while a finger is on it.

## Known

- Chromium's own processes (GPU, network and storage, about 80 MB together) still run from the first page opened until the app quits: Chromium cannot be stopped and started again inside the app. Giving all of it back with the last page needs the browser in a process of its own, which is planned (#117).
