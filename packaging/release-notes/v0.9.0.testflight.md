AgentTerm for iPhone is a remote for the coding agents running on your Mac. This version works with AgentTerm 0.8 and 0.9 on the Mac: https://github.com/manhpham90vn/AgentTerm/releases

New in 0.9.0

- A touch on an entry of a menu in the sessions' list (End Session, Rename Group, Pin, Move Down) is no longer, now and then, lost with the menu staying open.
- With the Mac's 0.9.0, a shell you use on the iPad is shown whole on the Mac, not cut off at the bottom.

Please try

1. Update the Mac app to 0.9.0 first, and restart its host when the app offers to.
2. In the sessions' list, touch and hold a session, then pick an entry of its menu, several times: each should do what it says, and the menu should go.
3. On an iPad, open a shell an agent opened on the Mac and type in it. On the Mac, look at that shell under its agent: the whole of it should be in sight, smaller, with its prompt.

What to tell me

What did not work, what was slow, and anything that read wrong.
